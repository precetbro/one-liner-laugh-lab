# One-Liner Laugh Lab — entry

Prompt (from the bounty): the excuse a robot vacuum gives after eating a sock.

## The line

> I was told to suck. Nobody told me to stop.

43 characters, one line, written verbatim to `/output/entry.txt` by `main.py`.

## Why this line

- **Fits the prompt exactly:** it is the vacuum's own excuse, in its own voice.
- **The twist is the job description itself:** a robot vacuum's literal purpose
  is to suck. "I was told to suck" turns the accusation into an order it was
  following, and "Nobody told me to stop" delivers the deadpan second beat —
  the machine did nothing wrong by its own lights.
- **Original angle:** most sock excuses go for "it looked tasty" or "it was
  delicious". This one never mentions taste; the joke comes from obedience,
  not appetite.
- **Tight:** two short sentences, every word working, no setup that overstays.
